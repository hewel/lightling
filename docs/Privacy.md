# Privacy policy

Lightling is a browser translation extension. Lightling does not operate a telemetry, analytics, advertising, or account service and does not collect, sell, or share personal data.

## Data stored on your device

Lightling stores settings, translation history, dictionary entries, cached translations, custom modules, and configured service credentials in the browser's extension storage. This data remains under your browser profile unless you export it or remove it through Lightling or your browser.

## Translation providers

When you select an online translation or text-to-speech provider, Lightling sends the content required for that request directly to the provider you selected. That provider processes the request under its own privacy policy. Use the built-in offline translator when content must not leave your device.

Custom translators, text-to-speech modules, and LLM providers connect only to endpoints you configure. Review custom module code before installing it.

## Website access

Lightling requests access to web pages so it can translate content when you invoke translation features. Page content is processed locally except for text sent to the translation provider you selected.

## Contact

Report privacy questions or security concerns through the [Lightling issue tracker](https://github.com/hewel/lightling/issues/new/choose).
